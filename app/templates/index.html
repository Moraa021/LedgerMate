Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LedgerMate features, M-Pesa tracking, and subscriptions.

How does LedgerMate track M-Pesa transactions?
When adding an entry or generating payment links with Paystack, you can input or receive the M-Pesa reference code (e.g. QK89D2L19). LedgerMate stores this metadata, eliminates duplicate entries, and connects it directly to your category reports.
Can I export reports for my accountant or tax filing?
Yes! LedgerMate allows you to export Profit & Loss statements, transaction histories, and Balance Sheets into PDF, CSV, and Excel with a single click.
Is there a free trial for the Pro plan?
Yes! You can start a 14-day free trial of our Pro plan with no credit card required. You can also use our free Starter plan forever with basic bookkeeping tools.
How does the inventory stock tracking work?
You can add inventory items with their unit cost, selling price, and reorder levels. When stock runs low, the system highlights items requiring restocking and calculates your total asset valuation in real time.
Can I access LedgerMate on my smartphone?
Absolutely. LedgerMate is built with a responsive web design and mobile-optimized touch controls, giving you a native app feel on iOS, Android, and tablets.
